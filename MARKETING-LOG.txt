1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & VALUE PROPOSITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
crm-cosmology (research-line/crm-cosmology) is an open-science research codebase
and bilingual preprint series developing the Curvature Relaxation Model (CRM),
a geometric modified-gravity cosmology program addressing dark-energy and
dark-matter phenomenology through curvature relaxation, scalaron dynamics,
and a MOND-oriented vector sector.

Key Value Propositions:
- Competitive Cosmological Fits: Native crm_fR model yields Delta chi2 = -3.7
  relative to standard LCDM on Planck 2018 CMB TT+TE+EE data in MCMC best-fit runs.
- Unified Geometric Alternative: Explores curvature relaxation as a unified
  replacement for independent dark sectors (dark matter and dark energy).
- Saturation Theorem Rigor: Rigorous mathematical classification of non-linear
  tanh-type saturation dynamics, smooth collar classifications, and source-bound
  projective D' candidate gates.
- Boltzmann Code Integration: Turnkey patch for hi_class (Horndeski in CLASS)
  implementing the native crm_fR gravity model with 3 physical parameters.
- Multi-Epoch Observational Confrontation: Quantitative reproduction scripts
  for Planck 2018 CMB, Pantheon+ SN Ia, and SPARC galaxy rotation curves (171 galaxies).
- Zero-Egress Privacy & Open Reproducibility: 100% offline local-first execution,
  unprivileged RunAsInvoker operation, zero telemetry, and permanent Zenodo anchors.
- Bilingual Preprint Parity: Complete 15-point quick navigation, dual Mermaid
  architecture diagrams, and synchronized documentation across English and German.

6. GOVERNANCE & RESEARCH INVARIANTS (INV-DET-01 to INV-SLA-10)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INV-DET-01: Deterministic Numerical Verification
  All statistical gates, MCMC seeds, and ODE solvers produce reproducible
  numerical certificates matching machine-readable JSON/CSV ledgers.
INV-ZERO-02: 100% Offline & Zero-Egress Privacy
  Zero outbound network calls, zero telemetry, and zero third-party cloud dependencies
  during computation, analysis, and test suite execution.
INV-USER-03: Unprivileged Execution / RunAsInvoker
  All code runs without root or administrative privileges, respecting standard
  POSIX/Windows user-space isolation.
INV-DATA-04: Curated Evidence Non-Pollution Boundary
  Downloaded observational raw data (Planck, Pantheon, SPARC) remain isolated
  under data/raw/ and are strictly gitignored to maintain a clean repository tree.
INV-GATE-05: Fail-Closed Gate Architecture
  Mathematical proof gates and D' candidate verifiers fail closed: incomplete
  or non-conclusive results are transparently audited as open research gates.
INV-ARCH-06: Dual-Format Bilingual Paper Series & Zenodo Archival
  Preprint manuscripts are maintained in LaTeX source and compiled PDF across
  both English and German, tied to immutable Zenodo DOI records.
INV-PLAT-07: Cross-Platform Environment Parity
  Verified seamless execution across Windows, Linux (Ubuntu/WSL), and macOS,
  with normalized CRLF/LF handling and robust path abstractions.
INV-OPEN-08: Permissive Open-Science CC-BY-4.0 & MIT Licensing
  All original manuscripts, figures, and code are distributed under permissive
  open-science terms ensuring unrestricted academic reproducibility.
INV-LLM-09: Machine-Readable LLM Parity
  Complete context indexing via standardized llms.txt, structured JSON schemas,
  and comprehensive 15-point bidirectional quick navigation anchors.
INV-SLA-10: 48-Hour Security Response & 5-Day Triage SLA
