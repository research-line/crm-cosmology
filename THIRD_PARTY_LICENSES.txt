## Zero-Copyleft Isolation & Unprivileged Execution Guarantee

- Zero-Copyleft Isolation: All original research code, scripts, verification harnesses, and preprint manuscripts in this repository remain independent original works distributed under the CC-BY-4.0 / MIT-compatible terms. All external runtime dependencies utilize permissive open-source licenses (PSFL-2.0, BSD-3-Clause, MIT, Apache-2.0, Matplotlib License), guaranteeing zero viral copyleft contamination of the research codebase or derivative scientific workflows.
- Zero-Egress Offline Guarantee: 100% offline, local-first execution with zero outbound network calls, telemetry, or third-party cloud data transmission.
- Unprivileged User-Mode Operation (RunAsInvoker): All scripts, CLI harnesses, and automated verification suites operate strictly within non-elevated user-mode privilege boundaries (INV-USER-03 / RunAsInvoker) without requiring administrative or root elevation.

## Governance & Runtime Invariants

crm-cosmology strictly enforces ten foundational research and architectural invariants:
- INV-DET-01: Deterministic Numerical Verification (deterministic seeds, ODE tolerances, and proof scripts matching stored certificates)
- INV-ZERO-02: 100% Offline & Zero-Egress Privacy (complete offline execution with zero telemetry, background calls, or cloud APIs)
- INV-USER-03: Unprivileged Execution / RunAsInvoker (strictly unprivileged user-mode execution without administrative elevation)
- INV-DATA-04: Curated Evidence Non-Pollution Boundary (strict .gitignore boundaries isolating external raw datasets and working notes)
- INV-GATE-05: Fail-Closed Gate Architecture (mathematical proof gates fail closed upon uncertainty; non-closed gates audited)
- INV-ARCH-06: Bilingual Paper Series & Zenodo Archival (preprints maintained in LaTeX & PDF across EN/DE, anchored to Zenodo DOIs)
- INV-PLAT-07: Cross-Platform Environment Parity (identical execution across Windows, Linux, and macOS)
- INV-OPEN-08: Permissive Open-Science CC-BY-4.0 (unrestricted scholarly reproducibility and research code reuse)
- INV-LLM-09: Machine-Readable LLM Parity (structured discovery via llms.txt and bilingual quick navigation)
- INV-SLA-10: 48-Hour Security Response & 5-Day Triage (committed response window for vulnerability reporting in SECURITY.md)
